About Josiah

What This Is

Clarity about the work.

Josiah holds a Master's degree in Mental Health Counseling, and the work draws on that training — but it is not therapy, and Josiah is not a licensed therapist. He does not assess, diagnose, or treat mental disorders. Instead, he provides guided reflection, practical skills training, and evidence-informed strategies to help clients navigate real-life challenges, develop greater self-awareness, strengthen resilience, and build healthier patterns of thinking and behavior.

For many people — especially teenagers, young adults, and men — this is exactly the kind of support that fits: structured, honest, practical, and free of the labels and paperwork that can make traditional clinical settings feel heavier than they need to be.

If you're experiencing a mental health crisis, believe you may have a mental health disorder that requires clinical assessment or treatment, or need urgent care, please seek help from a licensed mental health provider or call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7.

My Approach

What happens
in a session.

Every person who walks in is different. The approach adjusts — the standard doesn't.

01
First, the full picture.
The first sessions aren't about solutions — they're about understanding. What you're carrying, where it started, what it's costing you. That foundation changes everything that comes after.
02
Then, clarity.
Once something is understood, it can be named. And once it's named, it loses some of its weight. That moment of recognition — of finally seeing something clearly — is where things begin to shift.
03
Then, the work.
Evidence-based and built around who you are. Every session has direction and every step forward belongs to you — not to a method or a model. The progress is yours to keep.
04
Until you have the tools to do it yourself.
The goal was never to diagnose or manage a condition indefinitely. It's to help you understand what you're facing, work through it, and walk away with the self-management tools to handle similar challenges on your own. That's what the work is actually for.
